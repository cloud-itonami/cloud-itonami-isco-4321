# Business Model: Independent Warehouse Stock Operations

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-4321`
- ISCO-08: `4321`
- Occupation: Stock Clerks
- Social impact: local-jobs, supply-reliability, waste-reduction

## Customer

- small warehouses
- co-op distribution centers
- local retailers

## Offer

- inventory receiving and shelving
- pick-list fulfillment
- cycle counting
- stock-out and shrinkage reporting
- operator training

## Revenue

- setup fee
- monthly stock-operations platform fee
- per-pick fee
- reporting package

## Trust Controls

- no robot dispatch near staffed aisles without governor gate
- count discrepancies are auditable, not editable
- stock-out alerts cannot be suppressed
